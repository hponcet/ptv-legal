PTV

Your Plex movies and TV shows on the big screen, with the remote.

PTV plays the movies and TV shows of your Plex server on your TV, with an interface designed for the remote control.

Features

  • Direct play first: your files play as they are when the TV supports them, without conversion by the server.
  • Choice of audio and subtitle tracks, preferred languages remembered, adjustable subtitles (size, font).
  • Resume where you left off, automatic next episode, skip intro and credits.
  • Quality adapted to your connection, including remote access.
  • Plex profiles, with PIN code.
  • Optional companion server to enrich the home screen (rows, lists, statistics).
